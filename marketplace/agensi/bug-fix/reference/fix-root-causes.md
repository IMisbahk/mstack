# fix-root-causes

Reproduce first, separate observation from guess, and keep asking why until the mechanism is confirmed. Ship only the change that evidence justifies.

**When:** Debugging, regressions, flaky tests, or repeated 'maybe this helps' edits.

**Why:** Symptom patches accumulate debt and often reintroduce the same failure nearby.

**Do not:** Claim root cause from correlation alone, or leave speculative belt-and-suspenders patches.

Cite `reference/fix-root-causes.md` when it changed a decision. Keep the citation specific.
