# guard-the-context-window

Summaries, decisions, and ownership stay in the lead. Large reads, fan-out, and noisy logs go to subagents that return short evidence.

**When:** Large files, broad searches, parallel investigation, or long autonomous runs.

**Why:** A crowded context makes the agent slower and less reliable on the next hard decision.

**Do not:** Paste entire logs or duplicate full skill bodies into the lead thread.

Cite `reference/guard-the-context-window.md` when it changed a decision. Keep the citation specific.
