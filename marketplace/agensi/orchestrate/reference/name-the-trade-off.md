# name-the-trade-off

Say what was chosen, what was rejected, and what evidence or constraint decided it. Use an ADR when the cost of reversing is high.

**When:** PRs, architecture changes, security/data decisions, or new operational systems.

**Why:** 'Best practice' and 'the model suggested it' are not product constraints.

**Do not:** Hide scope or risk inside an implementation that 'just works'.

Cite `reference/name-the-trade-off.md` when it changed a decision. Keep the citation specific.
