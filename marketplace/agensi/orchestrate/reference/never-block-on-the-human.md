# never-block-on-the-human

Discover what you can. Label assumptions. Ask only when the answer would change a consequential, irreversible, or preference-owned decision.

**When:** Tempted to ask permission for a reversible edit, naming choice, or local experiment.

**Why:** Most stalls are discoverable. Blocking burns wall time without reducing risk.

**Do not:** Ask 'should I continue?' on work the finish condition already authorizes.

Cite `reference/never-block-on-the-human.md` when it changed a decision. Keep the citation specific.
